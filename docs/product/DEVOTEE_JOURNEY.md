# Devotee Journey

## Purpose

This journey keeps VEDIKA's customer experience connected from first launch to a service, temple, or personal spiritual activity. Screens must distinguish catalogue information from verified live data.

## Primary journey

1. A first-time visitor chooses sign-in or guest exploration during onboarding.
2. Home offers city selection, search, service categories, and entry points to devotional tools.
3. Search and Explore help the visitor narrow the catalogue with clear filters and recover from empty results.
4. Service Detail explains what is being requested, the indicative price, preparation, and conditions before entering the existing booking flow.

## Supporting journeys

- Temple: Home or Explore to temple discovery, then verified information or an explicit unavailable state and official source when supplied.
- Panchang: Home to date and city selection, then trusted source data or an explicit unavailable state.
- Japa: Home to mantra selection and counter, with progress retained by the agreed state contract.
- Samagri and Katha: Home to catalogue discovery, then a clear next action without implying live stock or ticket availability.
- Nearby: a visitor explicitly chooses nearby discovery, then grants location permission or selects a city manually.

## Trust rules

- Do not display invented availability, ratings, provider verification, temple slots, inventory, religious timings, or event status.
- Every data-backed surface needs loading, empty, unavailable, and retry treatment supplied by the agreed architecture contract.
- Return actions preserve the user's search, filter, and selection context.
